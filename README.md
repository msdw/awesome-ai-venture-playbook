## Who This Is For

Built for:
- **First-time AI founders** — who have an idea and need a structured path to first customers
- **Indie hackers** — turning AI experiments into revenue-generating ventures
- **Product builders** — going from side project to validated AI product

---

## What This Is

A practical, anti-hype playbook repository for AI venture building. It covers:

- **AI ideas** — scored and structured starting points
- **Ventures** — real documented AI ventures with what worked and what didn't
- **Playbooks** — step-by-step launch guides (validate, build, sell, iterate)
- **Resources** — tools, templates, frameworks for AI founders

## What This Is Not

- Not financial advice or investment guidance
- Not get-rich-quick content
- Not a directory of affiliate links
- Not a list of guaranteed opportunities
