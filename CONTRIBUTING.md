# Contributing to Awesome AI Venture Playbook

Thank you for contributing. All submissions are reviewed by maintainers before merging.

## Ways to Contribute

### Suggest a venture

Open an issue using the [Suggest Venture template](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-venture-playbook/issues/new?template=suggest-venture.yml).

Include: what the venture does, who it targets, what stage it reached, what worked, what didn't.

### Suggest an idea

Open an issue using the [Suggest Idea template](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-venture-playbook/issues/new?template=suggest-idea.yml).

### Suggest a resource

Open an issue using the [Suggest Resource template](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-venture-playbook/issues/new?template=suggest-resource.yml).

### Report a problem

Open a [Problem Report](https://github.com/msdw/awesome-ai-venture-playbook/issues/new?template=report-problem.yml) for broken links, hype language, or outdated content.

## Quality Bar

### What gets accepted

- Ventures with honest outcome data (revenue, users, or "shut down after 3 months — here's why")
- Ideas connected to real market signals
- Resources that are genuinely useful to AI builders, not just popular

### What gets rejected

- "Guaranteed revenue" or "passive income" language
- Ventures without any outcome data
- Ideas that are too vague to evaluate
- Spam, affiliate links, or self-promotional content
